In 1947, engineers working on the Mark II computer at Harvard University found a <span color="yellow_bg">moth stuck in a relay.</span> They taped the insect in their logbook and labeled it "first actual case of bug being found."
The words "bug" and "debug" soon became a standard part of the language of computer programmers.
